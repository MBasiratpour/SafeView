SafeView is a browser add-on that hides, blurs, or warns about extremist propaganda the way SafeSearch handles explicit content — by reading context, not just keywords, so ordinary political speech is left alone.

Context, not keywords

The key difference from a simple keyword blocker is context. SafeView distinguishes between content that promotes or glorifies an ideology and content that discusses, reports on, researches, or criticizes it.

  • Protect children and teenagers from explicit extremist propaganda

  • Reduce accidental exposure on social media and websites
  • Avoid treating ordinary political opinions as extremist content

Four classification levels

A 0–100 internal risk score maps to one of four visible behaviors. Thresholds are meant to be validated through testing, not fixed in stone.

0

Normal Political Content

No action. Political discussion, election coverage, opinions, historical documentaries, criticism of extremist movements.

1

Potentially Problematic

Labeled with a warning but stays visible. The user chooses whether to continue.

2

Extremist Content

Blurred or hidden by default. "Show Content" is available if the user's settings allow it.

3

Explicit Propaganda

Recruitment, glorification, or support for violence — strongly hidden or fully blocked in Child Mode.

What SafeView should never block

A core principle is avoiding political censorship. The system targets extremist advocacy — not the presence of a right-wing topic.

  • Conservative or other non-extremist political opinions
  • Criticism of immigration, governments, or political parties
  • News reporting about extremist organizations
  • Historical or educational material
  • Academic research
  • Satire and commentary
  • Content that condemns or analyzes extremist ideology

System architecture

Analysis happens in layers, favoring on-device processing over sending browsing activity to a server.

Browser → Content Script → Text / Image / Video Analysis → Context Classifier → Risk Score → SAFE / WARN / BLUR / BLOCK → User Interface

Text analysis

Reads visible text, titles, descriptions, captions, and comments — with sentence-level context, not word matching.

Image analysis

Detects propaganda imagery and symbols in posters, memes, and photos, blurring above the user's risk threshold.

Video analysis

Combines title, description, thumbnail, captions, and transcript to catch content a neutral title would hide.

Page-level analysis

Estimates whether a whole page reads as informational, journalistic, or propagandistic overall.

Social platforms

Built for YouTube, Reddit, X, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Discord Web, and forums — flagged posts become a warning card, not a deletion.

Privacy-first

No personal browsing profile. What's processed remotely, if anything, is stated clearly to the user.

Protection modes

Minimal

Only highly confident extremist content is hidden.

Balanced Default

Suspicious content receives warnings or is blurred.

Strict

More potentially extremist propaganda is hidden automatically.

Child Mode

Strong filtering; settings are locked behind a parent or administrator.
